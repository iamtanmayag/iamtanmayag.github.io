THE CHALLENGE

Why this mattered

Insurance documents arrive in different layouts and formats, sometimes as large PDFs, nested email attachments or damaged files. Reliable extraction requires more than a model call: the document must be made usable, processing must finish within infrastructure limits and downstream data must be checked.

THE ENGINEERING

What I did

01

Recover and prepare

Worked on PDF repair, OCR rotation and recursive parsing of email and archive attachments to make complex inputs usable.

02

Integrate AI

Built and maintained Azure OpenAI GPT-4o document extraction and classification workflows, connecting OCR outputs, client-specific handling and structured validation.

03

Design for limits

Optimized concurrent Azure OCR requests with asyncio.gather and page-level resource management to control peak memory use within Lambda.

THE OUTCOME

What changed

In an affected PDF rotation workflow, processing that could exceed Lambda’s 15-minute limit was reduced to approximately 1–2 minutes in observed runs.

Ownership, precisely.

My work covered the backend workflows, integration, processing and validation. This is not a claim that I trained the model or developed every piece of core AI logic.
