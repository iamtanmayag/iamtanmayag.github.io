06 / FULL STACK · IOT 2024

One control plane for air curtains across India.

A paid, independently built web platform connecting a national air-curtain fleet to real-time monitoring and remote control.

MY ROLEEnd-to-end software development and deployment
SELECTED TOOLSReact · Node.js · Express · DynamoDB · AWS IoT · MQTT
06 / 06

Real engineering work, shared at a level that respects company and client confidentiality.

Discuss the work
THE CHALLENGE

Why this mattered

An engineering company wanted to monitor the operating data of its air curtains distributed across India and control devices from a central web portal.

THE ENGINEERING

What I did

01

Build the portal

Developed the React interface and Node.js/Express backend for authenticated device management, telemetry and location-based filters.

02

Connect the devices

Implemented AWS IoT Core/MQTT publishing and subscriptions using certificates provided by the hardware team.

03

Make data actionable

Integrated DynamoDB-backed telemetry and remote-control workflows and collaborated with the professor and hardware team during integration.

THE OUTCOME

What changed

The platform was deployed as a paid company project and, according to the company engagement, is used to manage devices across India.

Ownership, precisely.

I independently developed the software. Device firmware and hardware were owned by the hardware team.
